# Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

**1. Who we are.** FitAbtal is a workout app for iOS with an Apple Watch companion. Contact: [SUPPORT EMAIL].

**2. Account.** You sign in with Sign in with Apple, Google, or email. We receive your email or an identifier from the provider.

**3. Data stored in the cloud (Supabase).** Your workouts and sets, routines, custom exercises, favorites, exercise notes and goals, fitness tests, program progress, and body measurements. It is synced so it is available on your devices, and it works offline.

If you choose to submit a program for publishing, its contents and cover image are uploaded and may be publicly visible. Nothing is submitted unless you do it yourself.

**4. Data that stays on your device.** Progress photos, and Apple Health data (such as sleep and resting heart rate) used for recovery. These are never uploaded. Video links you add to your own exercises are stored only on your device.

**5. Videos.** Exercise demonstration videos are streamed from our CDN (Bunny). If you add a YouTube link to your own exercise, the video plays in YouTube's embedded player. Google may collect data (such as your IP address and device information) as described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). We do not send your account data to YouTube.

**6. Purchases.** Pro is optional. Apple processes payment and RevenueCat manages the subscription status. We never see your card details.

**7. Notifications.** Reminders are scheduled locally on your device.

**8. No ads, no tracking.** The app has no ads and no advertising or analytics SDKs.

**9. Retention and deletion.** We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account from Settings inside the app, which removes your cloud data. Device-only data is removed by deleting it or the app.

**10. Your rights.** You can export your data as CSV at any time, and you can ask us to correct or delete it.

**11. Children.** The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

**12. Changes.** We will update the date above when this policy changes.
